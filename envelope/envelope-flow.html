✗ Authentication is all or nothing
Wrong key, wrong AAD, one flipped bit, a chunk reordered, duplicated or dropped, a stream cut short: every one of them ends as an error, never as plaintext.
AAD is authenticated, never stored.
Sub-keys are wiped on return; the KEK and master key are yours to Zero().
Equal plaintexts never seal to equal bytes: fresh salt, fresh nonce, every time.
